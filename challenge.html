Eat One Fruit Per Day

Goal: Eat at least one serving of fruit every day for 7 days

0/7 days completed

🧠 Did You Know?

Eating a variety of fruits provides different vitamins and minerals. Each color of fruit offers unique health benefits! For example, orange fruits like mangoes are rich in Vitamin A for eye health, while berries are packed with antioxidants that protect your cells.

💪 Why This Challenge?

  • Fruits boost your immune system
  • Natural sugars give you energy
  • Fiber helps digestion
  • Vitamins support brain function for studying

Eat Different Colored Foods

Eat foods of at least 3 different colors each day

Why? Different colored foods contain different nutrients! Red foods have lycopene, orange has beta-carotene, green has chlorophyll, etc. Eating a rainbow ensures diverse nutrition.
Color examples:
Red: Tomato, Apple Orange: Carrot, Mango Green: Broccoli, Spinach Purple: Eggplant, Grapes White: Rice, Banana
