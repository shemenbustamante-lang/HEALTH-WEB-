Student Health Tips 💡

Simple, practical advice for busy students like you!

💧

Drink Water Regularly

Water is essential for your body and brain! Aim for 8 glasses a day.

Why it matters:

  • Helps you stay focused in class
  • Prevents headaches and fatigue
  • Keeps your skin healthy
  • Aids digestion

Easy tip: Carry a water bottle to school!

🍳

Don't Skip Meals

Especially breakfast! Your body needs regular fuel throughout the day.

Why it matters:

  • Maintains steady energy levels
  • Prevents overeating later
  • Supports concentration and memory
  • Keeps your metabolism healthy

Easy tip: Keep quick breakfast options ready (banana, bread, yogurt)!

🥗

Eat Fruits and Vegetables Daily

Try to include at least 5 servings of fruits and veggies each day.

Why it matters:

  • Packed with vitamins and minerals
  • Boosts your immune system
  • Provides fiber for digestion
  • Helps prevent diseases

Easy tip: Add fruits to breakfast and vegetables to lunch/dinner!

🍟

Avoid Too Much Junk Food

It's okay to enjoy treats sometimes, but don't make them your main meals.

Why it matters:

  • Junk food lacks important nutrients
  • High in unhealthy fats and sugar
  • Can make you feel sluggish
  • Doesn't keep you full for long

Easy tip: Choose junk food as occasional treats, not daily meals!

⚖️

Balance Food with Rest and Activity

Eating well is just one part - also get enough sleep and stay active!

Why it matters:

  • Exercise helps you stay healthy
  • Good sleep helps you learn better
  • Balance prevents burnout
  • Active body, active mind!

Easy tip: Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep and 30 minutes of activity daily!

🍽️

Practice Portion Control

Eat enough to feel satisfied, but not overly full.

Why it matters:

  • Prevents feeling too full or sluggish
  • Helps maintain healthy weight
  • Allows you to enjoy food without guilt
  • Better digestion

Easy tip: Use smaller plates and eat slowly to recognize when you're full!

🥤

Limit Sugary Drinks

Soda, sweetened tea, and energy drinks add empty calories.

Why it matters:

  • Sugar causes energy spikes and crashes
  • Not good for teeth
  • Provides no nutritional value
  • Can make you more hungry

Easy tip: Replace soda with water, fresh fruit juice, or milk!

🍎

Snack Smart Between Meals

Choose nutritious snacks instead of chips and candy.

Why it matters:

  • Keeps energy levels stable
  • Prevents extreme hunger
  • Provides extra nutrients
  • Helps you focus better

Easy tip: Pack fruits, nuts, or yogurt for healthy snacking!

🍜

Cook Simple Meals When Possible

Home-cooked meals are usually healthier and more affordable.

Why it matters:

  • You control ingredients and portions
  • Less salt and oil than restaurant food
  • More cost-effective
  • It's a useful life skill!

Easy tip: Start with simple recipes like fried rice or scrambled eggs!

🕐

Eat at Regular Times

Try to have meals around the same time each day.

Why it matters:

  • Helps your body develop a routine
  • Prevents extreme hunger or overeating
  • Better energy management
  • Improves digestion

Easy tip: Set reminder alarms for meal times if needed!

🧘

Eat Mindfully, Not While Distracted

Put away your phone and focus on your food.

Why it matters:

  • Helps you enjoy your food more
  • Recognize when you're full
  • Better digestion
  • More satisfying meals

Easy tip: Take breaks from studying to eat properly!

📝

Plan Your Meals Ahead

A little planning prevents last-minute unhealthy choices.

Why it matters:

  • Saves time and money
  • Reduces stress about what to eat
  • Ensures balanced nutrition
  • Less likely to skip meals

Easy tip: Plan your weekly meals every Sunday!
