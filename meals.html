Breakfast (Start Your Day Right)

Why breakfast is important:

  • ⚡ Gives you energy to start the day
  • 🧠 Improves focus and concentration in class
  • 💭 Boosts memory and learning ability
  • 😊 Better mood and less irritability

Healthy Breakfast Ideas:

🍚

🍌

🥣

Filling breakfast that keeps you satisfied

Fiber - Digestion Vitamins - Immunity Complex Carbs - Long Energy

Calcium - Bones Protein - Growth Vitamin C - Immunity
🥞

Carbs - Energy Potassium - Muscles B Vitamins - Metabolism

Key Nutrients in Breakfast:

  • Carbohydrates – Energy for studying and activities
  • Protein – Keeps you full longer, prevents mid-morning hunger
  • Vitamins – Boost focus, memory, and immunity

Lunch (Fuel for the Day)

Why lunch is important:

  • 🔋 Prevents afternoon tiredness and energy crashes
  • 📚 Helps you stay alert during afternoon classes
  • 🎯 Reduces hunger pangs that distract from learning
  • 💪 Provides nutrients for afternoon activities

Healthy Lunch Ideas:

🍗

Rice + Grilled Chicken + Vegetables

Balanced meal with complete nutrition

Protein - Muscle Fiber - Digestion Iron - Energy
🐟

Omega-3 - Brain Protein - Growth Vitamins - Health
🍲

Protein - Fullness Carbs - Energy Fiber - Digestion
🥘

Quick, affordable, and nutritious

Vitamins - Immunity Protein - Energy Fiber - Fullness
🍜

Warm, comforting, and nutritious

Hydration - Body Vitamins - Health Minerals - Function
🍱

Rice + Pork/Beef + Veggies

Hearty meal for active students

Iron - Blood Protein - Strength B Vitamins - Energy

Key Nutrients in Lunch:

  • Protein – Supports muscle and brain development
  • Fiber – Aids digestion and keeps you feeling full
  • Iron – Prevents fatigue and supports blood health

Snacks (Smart Snacking)

Smart snacking tips:

  • ✅ Snacks are okay if chosen wisely
  • 🕐 Choose snacks between meals to maintain energy
  • 🥤 Pair snacks with water for better hydration
  • 📏 Keep portions reasonable - not too much
