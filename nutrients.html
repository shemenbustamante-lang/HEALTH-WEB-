Learn What's Inside Your Food! 🔬

Every food has nutrients that help your body. Here's a simple guide:

🍞

Carbohydrates

What it does:

Gives you energy for studying, thinking, and activities. Your brain's main fuel!

Food sources:

  • Rice
  • Bread
  • Pasta
  • Potatoes
  • Fruits
  • Oatmeal

Fun fact:

💡 Complex carbs (like brown rice) give longer-lasting energy than simple sugars!

🍗

Protein

What it does:

Builds and repairs muscles, helps you grow, keeps you full longer, and supports your immune system.

Food sources:

  • Chicken
  • Fish
  • Eggs
  • Beans
  • Nuts
  • Milk & Yogurt

Fun fact:

💡 Your body can't store protein, so you need it every day!

🥑

Healthy Fats

What it does:

Supports brain function, helps absorb vitamins, provides long-lasting energy, and protects organs.

Food sources:

  • Avocado
  • Nuts
  • Olive oil
  • Fish (salmon)
  • Peanut butter
  • Seeds

Fun fact:

💡 Not all fats are bad! Your brain is 60% fat.

🥕

Fiber

What it does:

Helps digestion, keeps you full, controls blood sugar, and promotes healthy gut.

Food sources:

  • Vegetables
  • Fruits
  • Whole grains
  • Beans
  • Oats
  • Brown rice

Fun fact:

💡 Fiber makes you feel full without adding many calories!

🍊

Vitamin C

What it does:

Boosts immune system, helps heal wounds, protects cells, and helps absorb iron.

Food sources:

  • Oranges
  • Guava
  • Strawberries
  • Tomatoes
  • Bell peppers
  • Broccoli

Fun fact:

💡 Your body can't store Vitamin C, so eat it daily!

🥛

Calcium

What it does:

Builds strong bones and teeth, helps muscles work, supports heart function.

Food sources:

  • Milk
  • Cheese
  • Yogurt
  • Tofu
  • Green leafy vegetables
  • Sardines

Fun fact:

💡 99% of your body's calcium is in your bones and teeth!

🥩

Iron

What it does:

Carries oxygen in blood, prevents fatigue, supports brain function and energy levels.

Food sources:

  • Red meat
  • Chicken
  • Fish
  • Beans
  • Spinach
  • Eggs

Fun fact:

💡 Eat Vitamin C with iron to absorb it better!

🥜

B Vitamins

What it does:

Converts food to energy, supports brain function, helps make red blood cells.

Food sources:

  • Whole grains
  • Meat
  • Eggs
  • Beans
  • Nuts
  • Green vegetables

Fun fact:

💡 There are 8 different B vitamins, each with unique jobs!

🥕

Vitamin A

What it does:

Supports good vision, healthy skin, strong immune system, and cell growth.

Food sources:

  • Carrots
  • Sweet potatoes
  • Squash
  • Mango
  • Spinach
  • Eggs

Fun fact:

💡 Carrots really do help your eyesight!

💧

Water

What it does:

Hydrates body, regulates temperature, transports nutrients, removes waste, helps concentration.

Food sources:

  • Plain water
  • Fruits (watermelon)
  • Vegetables (cucumber)
  • Soups
  • Coconut water

Fun fact:

💡 Your body is about 60% water! Drink 8 glasses daily.

🍌

Potassium

What it does:

Helps muscles contract, supports heart function, regulates blood pressure, maintains fluid balance.

Food sources:

  • Bananas
  • Potatoes
  • Beans
  • Avocado
  • Spinach
  • Orange juice

Fun fact:

💡 Prevents muscle cramps during sports and activities!

🐟

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

What it does:

Supports brain health, improves memory and focus, reduces inflammation, supports heart health.

Food sources:

  • Fish (salmon, tuna)
  • Walnuts
  • Flaxseeds
  • Chia seeds
  • Sardines

Fun fact:

💡 Called "brain food" - helps you study better!

💪 Powerful Nutrient Combinations

Some nutrients work better together!

Iron + Vitamin C

🥩 + 🍊 = Better iron absorption

Example: Meat with tomatoes

Calcium + Vitamin D

🥛 + ☀️ = Stronger bones

Example: Milk + sunlight exposure

Carbs + Protein

🍞 + 🥚 = Sustained energy

Example: Bread with eggs

Healthy Fats + Vitamins

🥑 + 🥕 = Better vitamin absorption

Example: Salad with olive oil
