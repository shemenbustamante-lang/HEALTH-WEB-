How Healthy Are Your Eating Habits? 🔍

Answer a few questions honestly to see how your current meals and snacks measure up. At the end, you'll get simple suggestions to improve your eating habits.

1. How often do you eat breakfast?

2. How many servings of fruits and vegetables do you eat daily?

3. How often do you eat chips, candies, or fast food?

4. How much water do you drink per day?

5. How often do you skip meals?

6. Do you include protein (meat, fish, eggs, beans) in your meals?

7. How often do you drink sugary beverages (soda, sweetened juice)?

8. Do you eat late at night (after 9 PM)?

9. How balanced are your meals (mix of carbs, protein, vegetables)?

10. Do you pay attention to portion sizes?
